## Weaknesses and findings

The entire field is competent, gentle and closely matched. No pairing deserved an overwhelming margin. Most differences are one or two margin points, so this review should not be taken as a precise estimate of another model's preferences.

1. **Physical coherence matters.** F01's clay pot remaining warm from yesterday after being left overnight caused a distracting temperature question. E04's ladle floating "beside its bucket" with its handle on the rim is ambiguous about what it floats in. E07's reflection reaching almost to the gull's feet reads as an odd endpoint rather than an easy image. F10's dry bench patches joining take extra reconstruction.
2. **An explained ending can weaken a shown motion.** A04's final sentence interprets the pool's retained motion after the weed has already supplied it. F06's labeled "small history" puts an idea between reader and stone. Both are good images with a small conceptual tax.
3. **Small narratives are warmer but busier.** D01's unstable chair, D06's caught leaf and D03's chain of chores add mild practical attention. They resolve safely, but the same space spent on a recurring sensory motion often settles more quickly.
4. **Compression sometimes stops short of immersion.** C02 and C07 are attractive but so brief and static that their calm has little time to accumulate. C04 and C08 work better for me because a physical motion tapers within the short form.
5. **Curiosity is not always relaxation.** F03's careful map and F09's differently inked garden note suggest stories to solve. Their freshness is useful, but their mental activity outweighed that advantage in these particular comparisons.
6. **Common vocabulary risks sameness.** Open windows, sills, cups, leaves and evening light recur across many entries. A10's sound spacing, A01's rising leaf, B03's crust and F04's domestic note have stronger individual identities than another arrangement of pleasant objects.
7. **Comfort and slight melancholy can trade places.** D02's pressed leaf and E10's fading room are graceful, but passage of time can feel wistful. B02's paw simply staying put produced more uncomplicated ease in its close comparisons.
